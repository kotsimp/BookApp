Με ➕ Ονόματα στο Γλωσσάρι η ελληνική τους γραφή περνά στο Γλωσσάρι, ώστε η μετάφραση να τα γράφει παντού ίδια.
      • 📑 Ανά κεφάλαιο: λίγες γραμμές για κάθε κεφάλαιο ή μέρος του βιβλίου, με τις σελίδες του. Ένα πολύ μεγάλο μέρος χωρίζεται σε κομμάτια, π.χ. «(2/4)». Με 📖 Ένα κεφάλαιο διαλέγετε ένα μόνο κεφάλαιο και πατάτε 📖 Αναλυτικά: τι συμβαίνει, ποιοι εμφανίζονται, πώς τελειώνει.
      • 🧭 Η ιστορία ως τώρα: γράψτε ως ποια σελίδα (ή κεφάλαιο) έχετε διαβάσει και πατήστε 🧭 Ανακεφαλαίωση. Γράφεται μόνο από αυτές τις σελίδες.
      • 📣 Οπισθόφυλλο: κείμενο που δεν αποκαλύπτει το τέλος. Γίνεται και η περιγραφή του βιβλίου στον κατάλογο του 📲 Συσκευές.
      • ❝ Αποσπάσματα: σημαντικά αποσπάσματα με τη σελίδα τους. Όποιο δεν βρίσκεται λέξη προς λέξη στο βιβλίο αφαιρείται.
      • 🎓 Οδηγός μελέτης: θέματα, ερωτήσεις για συζήτηση, κουίζ και απαντήσεις.
      • 🕰️ Χρονολόγιο: τα γεγονότα με τη σειρά που συνέβησαν, όχι με τη σειρά που αφηγούνται.

      The app translates books and documents (English ⇄ Greek), makes audiobooks and writes summaries. Jobs run on the server: you can close the page and come back later.

      📖 Quick guide

      🌐 Translation

      1. Upload a PDF, DOCX, EPUB file or a CBZ / CBR comic. Several files at once go into a queue and are translated one after another.
      2. ⚡ Basic Mode: press 🌐 Start Translation and the app picks the language, format and layout itself.
        ⚙️ Advanced Mode: you choose the direction, the file format, the layout (Document, Literature, Graphic Novel), the cover, AI Polish and Maximum quality.
      3. Above the button, the 📊 Estimate says how many Gemini requests are needed and whether they fit your keys' daily limit. 🧪 Translate the sample translates only a few pages (e.g. 1–10), so you can see the style before the whole book.
      4. The progress card shows the step, the percentage and the time left.
      5. When it is done, the green button downloads the file and the small buttons give the other formats (PDF, DOCX, EPUB, TXT). 📖 Read now opens it in the browser, and ✏️ Review & Edit lets you correct the translation next to the original. 🔀 Bilingual gives the original and the translation together, paragraph by paragraph.

      🎧 Audiobook

      Upload a PDF, DOCX or EPUB, choose a voice and a narration pace, and start. Listen in the built-in player or download it as MP3 or as M4B with chapters. With 📖 Text the text lights up as it is read; press a sentence to go there.

      💡 Book Insights

      A short summary or a full synopsis of a book. Under 🔎 More about the book you choose the Text language and one of these:

      • 👥 Characters & terms: who is who and the key terms, with the page where each first appears. Names the book does not contain are dropped, as are the author and people only quoted (e.g. in epigraphs). ➕ Names to the Glossary puts their Greek spelling in the Glossary, so a translation writes them the same everywhere.
      • 📑 Chapter by chapter: a few lines for each chapter or part of the book, with its pages. A very long part comes in pieces, e.g. “(2/4)”. With 📖 One chapter you choose just one chapter and press 📖 In detail: what happens, who appears, how it ends.
      • 🧭 The story so far: write the page (or chapter) you have read up to and press 🧭 Recap. It is written from those pages only.
      • 📣 Back-cover blurb: a text that keeps the ending secret. It is also the book's description in the 📲 Devices catalogue.
      • ❝ Quotes: memorable passages with their page. Any that is not in the book word for word is dropped.
      • 🎓 Study guide: themes, discussion questions, a quiz and its answers.
      • 🕰️ Timeline: the events in the order they happened, not the order they are told.
      • 💬 Ask the book: answers from the text only, with the pages they rest on.

      Every result shows on screen, downloads as PDF, DOCX or TXT, can be heard with 🎧 Create audio and is kept with the book. It needs a Gemini key.

      🔑 Gemini keys

      AI translation uses Google Gemini. A free key is made at aistudio.google.com/apikey, and the administrator adds it under 🔑 Gemini API Key. Up to 8 keys fit, and they take turns.

      • ΕΛ | EN: the app's language. The text of your books does not change.
      • ☀️ / 🌙: the light theme by day, the dark one at night; press it to switch.
      • 📚 Glossary: a fixed translation for names and terms across the whole book.
      • 🕑 Session History: download again or continue earlier jobs.
      • 📲 Devices: a catalogue for e-reader apps (OPDS).

      💬 Frequently asked questions

      Do I need a Gemini key?

      Not necessarily. Without a key the app uses Google's free translation and then MyMemory, with lower quality and small limits (Google often refuses long texts). With a free Gemini key the translation is done by AI and is much better. Book Insights need a key.

      Why does a translation take hours?

      Almost always because of the free Gemini limits (requests per minute and per day), not because of your computer or server. Maximum quality adds a second pass of about 3–8 seconds per page. More keys from different Google accounts speed it up.

      What happens when the Gemini daily limit runs out?

      With ⏳ Wait for the reset (the default) the job stops safely where it got to. The limit renews every day at midnight California time (around 10:00 in Greece). Queued jobs then continue by themselves, and so does a single job if its page is open; otherwise press ▶️ Resume. With 🔄 Switch to the free model automatically the job carries on at once with the free engine, at lower quality. The keys window shows what is waiting and until when.

      Do several keys from the same Google account help?

      Little or not at all. Google counts the limits per project, so keys of the same project share one limit. For more capacity, add keys from different Google accounts.

      Is the translation lost if I close the page or the server restarts?

      No. The job runs on the server and its progress is saved all the time. After a restart the 📌 Unfinished job at a checkpoint notice appears: press ▶️ Resume NOW and it continues from where it stopped.

      How accurate is the time left?

      It comes from the pace over the last few minutes, so it settles about a minute after the page opens. It hides while the job waits for the Gemini limit. With Maximum quality the second pass follows and shows its own time.

      What does “Maximum quality” do?

      A second AI pass compares every page of the translation with the original and corrects errors of meaning, omissions and vocabulary, like a second editor. It is slow and uses more of the daily limit. If it does not complete, the first-pass translation is kept.

      Can I try a few pages first?

      Yes: 🧪 Translate the sample translates only the pages you choose (chapters, for an EPUB), with the same settings. The 📊 Estimate says how many Gemini requests the sample needs, and how many the whole book. If you like it, press 🌐 Translate the whole book. The sample's file downloads with “_sample” in its name.

      Can I ask questions about a book?

      Yes: in 💡 Book Insights → 💬 Ask the book. The answer comes only from the book's text, with the pages it rests on; when the book does not answer it, it says so. The questions are kept with the book; 🗑️ next to a question deletes it, and “🗑️ Clear All” deletes them all. Each question sends the whole book in one large Gemini request. A very long book sends its related parts: the question first becomes search words in the book's language, so you can ask in Greek about an English book. If you made “Chapter by chapter”, it goes along as an overview.

      How does “Chapter by chapter” find the chapters?

      In an EPUB it takes its chapters. In a PDF, DOCX or TXT it uses the PDF's bookmarks, the table of contents (even without page numbers) and headings such as “Chapter 3” or “Prologue”; when it finds none, it cuts the book into equal parts. A very long part, as in novels with a few long parts, comes in pieces of about 30 pages, so all of it is summarised. Acknowledgments, licences, contents and indexes are left out.

      Will “The story so far” give away what comes next?

      No. Only the pages up to the one you wrote are sent to Gemini, so it does not know what follows. The 📣 Back-cover blurb keeps the ending secret too. The others (summaries, chapter by chapter, timeline) cover the whole book.

      How many Gemini requests do Book Insights need?

      A short book needs a few. A long novel is read section by section, so it needs more: e.g. about 6 for “Chapter by chapter” on a 300-page book. Each question to the book is one large request. With several Gemini keys the sections are read at the same time, two per key: the same requests in much less time. Results are kept with the book, so opening them again costs no requests.

      Can I read the books on an e-reader or tablet?

      Yes: in 📲 Devices press 🔗 New link and add it as an OPDS catalogue in an app such as KOReader or Moon+ Reader. You see your finished books and download them as EPUB, PDF or bilingual EPUB (comics as CBZ, EPUB or PDF). A comic's EPUB and CBZ are made when the catalogue opens, as sharp as its scans (about 1.2 MB a page): if a download stays on “Downloading…”, open the catalogue again, wait a minute and download it again. The link is secret; a new one stops the old.

      Can a scanned PDF (only pictures of pages) be translated?

      Yes, without OCR. On upload the app asks what it holds and suggests an answer:

      📖 Book: Gemini Vision first reads the text of every page (one request per page, on a light model), then it is translated like a digital book, in any format you like. The text read also serves audiobooks and Book Insights, which read it themselves when needed.
      🎨 Comic: the translation is drawn onto the pages themselves, in the balloons.
      Both need a Gemini key.

      A name is translated differently each time.

      Add it to the 📚 Glossary with the translation you want; it is kept across the whole book.

      Where do my files go?

      Files and translations stay on the server where the app runs until you delete them from the History. For translation the text is sent to Google (Gemini or Google Translate) or to MyMemory, and for audiobooks to Microsoft's voice service (Edge TTS). With user accounts, everyone sees only their own jobs, and administrators see all of them.

      I forgot my password.

      An administrator presses 🔑 Reset password under 👥 Users and gives you a temporary password. You change your password from the 👤 button at the top right.

      How do I put the app on my iPhone home screen?

      In Safari press Share → Add to Home Screen. It appears with the app's icon. On an iPhone or iPad, every file you download (a translation, an audiobook, Book Insights) opens the Share sheet: choose Save to Files, Books and so on, and the app stays where it was. If the ✅ The file is ready bar appears, press 📤 Save or share.

      After an update I still see the old look.

      Press 🔄 in the tab bar. If that is not enough, reload with Ctrl+F5 (on a phone, close the tab and open it again).
